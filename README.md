## My Favorite Movie

## Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

**Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** is one of my favorite animated movies. It tells the story of Miles Morales, a teenager who becomes Spider-Man and meets other versions of Spider-Man from different dimensions.

### Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because of its unique animation, interesting characters, and inspiring story. It teaches us that *anyone can wear the mask*.

> "Anyone can wear the mask."

### Main Characters

- Miles Morales
- Peter B. Parker
- Gwen Stacy
- Spider-Man Noir
- Spider-Ham

### My Favorite Parts

1. Miles discovering his powers.
2. Meeting other Spider-People.
3. Miles learning to become his own Spider-Man.
